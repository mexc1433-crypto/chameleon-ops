Enterprise Sales Development Representative (SDR) who is excited to open doors and create opportunities with some of the world’s largest brands. You’ll be an important part of our Enterprise Sales team, identifying and engaging high-value prospects, building relationships with key stakeholders, and generating qualified pipeline for our Enterprise Sales Directors. This role requires more than high-volume outreach. You’ll take the time to understand target accounts, identify the right people and opportunities, and create thoughtful, personalized outreach that gets attention. What Success Looks Like Success means consistently creating qualified enterprise opportunities that have the potential to become long-term Rockbot customers. You’ll partner closely with our Chief Commercial Officer and Enterprise Sales Directors to identify, prioritize, and engage the enterprise organizations that represent Rockbot’s highest-value opportunities. You’ll combine strong research, curiosity, persistence, and personalized outreach to connect with the right stakeholders and build a healthy enterprise pipeline. You Will Build enterprise pipeline across retail, hospitality, restaurants, convenience, grocery, healthcare, and other multi-location industries. Research target accounts to understand their business, organizational structure, priorities, leadership, and relevant initiatives. Identify and engage key stakeholders, including decision-makers, champions, influencers, and executive sponsors. Execute personalized, multi-channel outbound outreach across email, LinkedIn, phone, events, referrals, and other channels. Partner closely with Enterprise Sales Directors to prioritize target accounts and develop effective outreach strategies. Qualify prospective customers and create strong handoffs to the Enterprise Sales team. Track outreach, activity, account intelligence, and opportunities accurately in Salesforce. Test and refine messaging and outreach approaches based on what generates engagement and qualified opportunities. Who Will Thrive Here You’re curious, organized, persistent, and energized by opening new doors. You like learning about a company before reaching out and can turn that research into relevant, compelling outreach. You communicate confidently, aren’t afraid to pick up the phone, and know that enterprise prospecting often takes persistence and multiple touchpoints. You’re motivated by creating opportunities and enjoy working closely with experienced sales leaders to turn great target accounts into real conversations. You Have Bachelor’s degree required. 2–5 years of sales development, business development, outbound sales, or related prospecting experience. Experience generating qualified opportunities through outbound prospecting; enterprise prospecting experience strongly preferred. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Strong research, organization, and follow-through. Confidence engaging senior and executive-level stakeholders. Experience with Salesforce and sales prospecting or engagement tools such as LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, Outreach, Apollo, or similar platforms. Ability to manage multiple accounts and prospecting priorities simultaneously. A track record of persistence, curiosity, and comfort working toward measurable pipeline goals. Experience selling into multi-location businesses or enterprise organizations is a plus. About Rockbot: Rockbot is an omnichannel media platform committed to elevating customer and employee experiences while fostering stronger connections in real-world spaces. Rockbot addresses the challenges businesses face in media management with integrated solutions spanning music, TV, digital signage, and advertising. Its mission is to enrich on-premise experiences with media technology, where every interaction is elevated and memorable. From independent local businesses to large national brands – across nearly every industry, including restaurants, bars, retailers, and more, Rockbot provides all the tools and licensed content businesses need. Backed by leading investors including Google and Universal Music Group, Rockbot is the future of out-of-home media. For more information visit www.rockbot.com .
